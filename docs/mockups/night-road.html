Life on the Night road

Your home screen already has a language: dark glass, cream marks, one lit colour, a road running to the horizon. This puts Life inside it. The road stops being wallpaper and becomes the picture of how far you have come: every centre dash is a day, lamps stand at milestones, and the tail lights are you.

Lock-screen page, four moments

One widget that changes with the moment. Every button here works without unlocking. Labels stay neutral, so the screen tells a stranger nothing.

The app, same language

One Today screen instead of five tabs. The dock is round glass, like your phone's. Each tile carries one light: teal for practice, amber for upkeep, violet for what you've committed to.
